Equities Bonds & cash

Sequence-of-returns risk.The five years before and after retirement do the most damage if a downturn hits while you're withdrawing — the same market drop hurts far more once you've stopped adding to the account.

A bucketed withdrawal.Cash and short bonds cover two to three years of spending, so equities are never sold to fund a distribution during a downturn — they're sold on your schedule, not the market's.

How I help

Five ways I can help.

Each service below stands on its own. Most people who come to me need exactly one — so that's all we'll talk about, unless you ask for more.

01 EST

Comprehensive estate planning

This is about deciding, in writing, what happens to your money, your house, and your stuff after you die — so your family knows exactly what to do instead of guessing or arguing about it.

02 EXEC

Executive benefits consulting & implementation

Some companies want to give their top leaders extra rewards on top of normal pay and a normal 401(k). I help the company design that special reward plan and get it up and running correctly.

03 LIFE

Life insurance consulting & procurement

Life insurance is a promise: if something happens to you, your family gets a check so they're okay financially. I help you compare policies and pick the right one, then handle the paperwork to get it in place.

04 ANN

Annuity consulting & procurement

An annuity is like putting money in now so it pays you back a little bit every month later, kind of like a paycheck after you stop working. I help you compare the options and buy the one that fits.

05 FIN

Final expense cost management

This is a small, simple insurance policy that just covers the cost of a funeral, so that bill doesn't land on your family while they're already grieving.

About
